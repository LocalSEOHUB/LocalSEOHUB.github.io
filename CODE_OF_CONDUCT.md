# Code of conduct

Comments on this blog are [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/LocalSEOHUB/LocalSEOHUB.github.io/discussions).
They are public, attached to your GitHub account, and moderated by the blog's author.

## The short version

Argue with the post, not the person.

## What is welcome

- Disagreement, including blunt disagreement. Being told you are wrong is the main
  reason to publish in the open.
- Corrections. Factual errors get fixed and credited.
- Questions, including ones you think are obvious.
- Additions — a link, a counter-example, the thing the post missed.

## What is not

- Personal attacks, harassment, or comments about someone rather than their argument.
- Slurs or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or circumstances.
- Sexual content, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Spam, promotional posting, and off-topic derailing.

## Moderation

The author may edit, hide, lock or delete comments, and may block accounts, without
prior notice and without owing an explanation. This is a personal blog rather than a
public forum — the bar is "does this thread help readers", not "is this technically
permitted".

Hidden and deleted comments are handled through GitHub Discussions and are subject to
GitHub's own retention behaviour.

## Scope

This applies to Discussions, issues, pull requests and any other interaction in this
repository.

## Reporting

For something that needs attention, email the address on the
[contact page](https://localseohub.github.io/contact/) rather than replying in the
thread. Serious abuse can also be reported directly to GitHub at
<https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

For **security** issues specifically, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — please do not
post those publicly.
